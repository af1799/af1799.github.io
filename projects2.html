Cumulo-Climb

Cumulo-Climb is a game for the 2026–2027 DGA Summer Project. I implemented a dynamic, adjustable camera system, designed modular level chunks to support scalable level design, and created a continuous background system with seamless transitions between environments. I also developed a particle system to enhance visual feedback and gameplay experience. You can play the project on itch.io.
